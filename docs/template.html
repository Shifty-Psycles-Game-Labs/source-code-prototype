Abstract

The Never Ending Model proposes a unified physical—informational framework in which the universe is represented as a 4-dimensional manifold `M_4=R^3xxS` where the progression axis `S` is not physical time but a compressed projection of underlying entropic evolution. NEG interprets the apparent temporal ordering of events as a coarse-grained representation of monotonic entropy production, encoded through a progression parameter `lambda` satisfying. `dS/dlambda >= 0`. Physical fields are modeled as tensor fields on `M_4`, and the fundamental analytic condition Of the theory is harmonicity: a tensor field `T` is physically admissible in vacuum when `DeltaT = 0`, where `Delta` is the Laplace—Beltrami operator associated with themetric on `M_4 ` This single condition unifies vacuum electromagnetism, linearized gravity, and quantum amplitudes under a common geometric criterion.

The entropic axis is represented as `E` such that the extended manifold is `M_4 x E`.
